# Spec: footer (MY-189)

## Behavior

- Every page, and the 404, ends with the same footer after `main`: the logo, the four navigation links,
  the contacts, then a pill bar, then the author line.
- The logo is a link to `/` named "YourBank". The navigation is the header's: the current page's link has
  `aria-current="page"` and is drawn green.
- Contacts: the email opens the mail client, the phone dials, the address is plain text. Each has a green
  icon that a screen reader skips.
- The bar holds three round green social buttons at the left (Facebook, X, LinkedIn), the copyright
  "YourBank All Rights Reserved" in the middle and "Privacy Policy | Terms of Service" at the right. The
  socials open the networks' home pages in a new tab and are named by `aria-label`. Privacy Policy and
  Terms of Service are text: there are no such pages, so there is nothing to follow.
- Under the bar, a muted centred line: "Serhii Lisovyk · <current year>". The year is written by a script
  when the page loads; without JavaScript the line reads "Serhii Lisovyk".
- At 1920 and 1440 the contacts are a row and the bar is one row. Below 768 px the contacts and the bar
  stack into centred rows. Between the drawn widths the sizes are fluid; no horizontal scroll from 320.
- Every link is reachable by Tab and shows the global focus ring; hover applies only on devices that hover.

## Not changed

- The header, the page sections, the metadata.
