# Plan: Careers Our Benefits and the shared icon-card (MY-196)

Goal: the Benefits section of Careers on a new `icon-card` component that Security's "How We
Protect You" reuses without edits. No spec: no new interaction. No open questions. The design
file is out of reach, so the sources are `ai/design-system.md` and a screenshot of the layout.

## Changes

- `partials/components/icon-card.html`, block `.icon-card` (`__head`, `__image`, `__title`, `__text`).
  Parameters: `icon`, `title`, `text`, and an `inset` flag (modifier `icon-card--inset`). It is an
  `<article>`; the title is an `h3`; the icon is decorative (`alt=""`, `aria-hidden`).
- `styles/components/icon-card.css`: a `--grey-11` card with a 1 px `--grey-15` border and
  `--border-radius`, padding `--space-card`, the circle left of the title on one row, the text under
  it (`--space-card-gap`). The dotted decor `dots-fine.avif` sits in its top-left corner (existing
  file, baked over `--grey-11`; decision `light-decor-raster`).
- `--inset` is the Security card: `--grey-10` fill, `--border-radius-medium`, no decor. Security's shared
  dotted backdrop is the section's style, not the card's.
- `partials/sections/careers/benefits.html` (`.benefits`): `section-heading` ("Our" + "Benefits") and a
  grid of `icon-card` from `{{#each benefits}}`. `styles/sections/benefits.css`: two columns, one below
  768 px; the gap is a fluid token `--space-benefits-gap` (100 / 60 / 48).
- `data/benefits.js` → `benefits` (`icon`, `title`, `text`), wired into `context`.
- Four icons in `assets/images/icons/`: `compensation.svg`, `health.svg`, `retirement.svg`,
  `work-life.svg` — the shared circle (outer 98, inner 74, ring) with a hand-drawn stroke glyph, cleaned
  with SVGO. They are not the template's glyphs; they need a visual check against the design.
- Tokens: `--icon-card` (the product circle size), `--space-benefits-gap`.
- `careers.html`: the section after Values.

## Docs

- `ai/map.md`: the Careers page line, the partials lines (`icon-card`, `careers/benefits`), the data line.
- `ai/design-system.md`: the Benefits card values decided here (radius, circle size, gap token, decor).
- Rules, skills, decisions: nothing; no pattern changed.
