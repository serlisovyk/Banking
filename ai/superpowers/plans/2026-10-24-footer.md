# Plan: footer (MY-189)

Goal: one footer on all pages, as the design draws it. Behavior is in
`ai/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-footer.md`. No open questions.

## Author's answers

- Socials link to `https://facebook.com`, `https://x.com`, `https://linkedin.com`, in a new tab
  (`target="_blank" rel="noopener"`), each with an `aria-label`.
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are plain text, no links (record `footer-legal-text`).
- The footer also shows `Serhii Lisovyk · <year>`; the year is written in the browser (record
  `footer-year-in-browser`).
- The design is unavailable for this task: the sources are `ai/design-system.md` ("Pages", "Buttons", spacing
  table) and a screenshot of the Home footer. The design's email typo is replaced by `hello@yourbank.com`.

## Changes

- `data/contacts.js` (`contacts`: icon, text, optional `href`), `data/socials.js` (`socials`: name,
  `href`, icon), `data/footer.js` (`footer`: copyright, legal documents, author name); the three wired
  into `context` in `vite.config.js`.
- `partials/sections/shared/footer.html`: `<footer class="footer" data-footer>` with a `.container`; the logo
  link, a `nav` with `aria-label="Footer navigation"` looped from `navigation` with `aria-current` like the
  header, `address` with the contacts, the bar, the author line.
- `styles/sections/footer.css` and its import. New fluid tokens in `variables.css` for the rows from the
  design-system table: top and bottom padding, row gap, social circle size. Text is `--text-body`
  at weight 400 in white; the bar and dividers are `--grey-15`; the legal line `--grey-70`, the author line
  `--grey-60`. Structure changes only at `width < 768px`.
- Icons: small SVGs in `assets/images/icons/` (`mail`, `phone`, `pin` green; `facebook`, `x`, `linkedin`
  dark glyphs for the green circles), in the style of the existing ones.
- `FooterController` (`scripts/controllers/`, root `data-footer`, `start()` renders the year into
  `[data-footer-year]`), its constants in `constants.js`, a row in `main.js`.
- `<footer>` after `</main>` on the four pages; `dist/404.html` stays the copy of `dist/index.html`.

## Placement of the author line

Its own small, muted, centred row under the bar, at every width: it does not compete with the design's bar
and needs no rule per breakpoint.

## Docs

- `ai/map.md`: the footer partial and stylesheet, `FooterController`, the data files, the pages list.
- `ai/design-system.md`: the footer values decided here (dividers, bar, author line).
- `ai/rules/html.md`: the footer's navigation and the new-tab links already follow the rules; nothing to change
  unless a rule turns out wrong.
- Two decision records (above).
