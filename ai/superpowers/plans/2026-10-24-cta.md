# Plan: CTA block (MY-190)

Goal: one CTA partial for Home and Careers, texts from `data/`. No spec: no new interaction; the
button is a plain link. No open questions.

## Changes

- `partials/sections/shared/cta.html`, block `.cta` (`__inner`, `__content`, `__title`, `__text`,
  `__button`). It takes one context, an entry of `data/cta.js`: `titleStart` / `titleAccent` /
  `titleEnd` (as `section-heading`), `text`, `button: { label, href }`. A page calls it as
  `{{> sections/shared/cta cta.home }}`. Own title and text, not `section-heading`: the CTA is a
  card, not a heading block (the same as `page-hero`). The title is an `h2`.
- `data/cta.js` → `cta` (`home`, `careers`), wired into `context`. The Careers text is written for the
  site (the design has lorem ipsum there).
- The button is `<a class="button button--large">` (the hero-action size, `ai/design-system.md`). Its
  destination is decided in MY-195; for now both entries point at Our Products: `#products` on Home,
  `/#products` on Careers. Our Products gets `id="products"` as the target.
- `styles/sections/cta.css`, imported in `style.css`: a `--grey-11` panel with a 1 px `--grey-15` border and
  `--border-radius-small`, content and button in a row; below 1024 px a centred column. Padding is a fluid
  token `--space-cta` (80 / 60 / 30); the panel height follows the content.
- Decor: a new `decor/dots-fine.avif`, a fine dot grid fading from the top-left corner, baked over
  `--grey-11`, opaque, drawn at 2x (640 × 600, shown at 320 px), about 6.5 kB (decision
  `light-decor-raster`). `dots-grid.avif` does not fit: its dots are much larger and brighter.
- Home: one line after the FAQ in `src/index.html`; Testimonials (MY-194) go between them. Careers is
  wired by MY-198.

## Docs

- `ai/map.md`: the pages line, the partials line, the data line, the decor file in the assets line if listed.
- `ai/design-system.md`: the CTA section, the decor table, the padding token.
- `ai/skills/section.md`, rules, decisions: nothing; no pattern changed.
